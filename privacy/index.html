Privacy Policy

Last updated October 24, 2026

This policy explains what information Social Elite Marketing Group, LLC d/b/a Boris ("we") collects on hireboris.com and how we use it. How Boris handles your firm's client data once he's working for you is covered by the Boris Service Agreement.

What we collect

  • Forms: when you explore the demo, book a walkthrough, hire Boris or fill in the setup checklist, we collect what you type: your name, firm, email, phone, role, number of attorneys, state and your setup answers.
  • Checkout: payments are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects your card and billing address. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Referrals: if you arrive through a partner's link, we note which partner referred you.
  • Basic browser storage: the site remembers things like your demo access and your cart on your own device, so you don't have to type them twice. We don't use advertising cookies on hireboris.com.

How we use it

  • To give you demo access, set up and run Boris for your firm, bill you, and answer your questions.
  • To follow up about Boris. You can ask us to stop any time.

Who we share it with

  • Stripe processes payments.
  • CrowdSync, our customer system, stores your contact details and our notes.
  • The hosting and cloud providers that run this site and Boris.
  • If you were referred, the referring partner may learn that you signed up (not your answers or payment details).

We don't sell your information, and we don't share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Keeping and deleting it

We keep your information while you're a customer or prospect, and as long as the law requires after that. Ask us to see, correct or delete it any time.

Security

We use encrypted connections, keep payment keys and passwords out of the website, and limit who on our team can see your information.
