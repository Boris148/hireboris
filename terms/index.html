Boris Service Agreement

Last updated October 24, 2026

This agreement is between Social Elite Marketing Group, LLC, a Texas limited liability company doing business as Boris ("we", "us"), and the law firm that signs up for Boris ("you", "your firm"). By checking the box at checkout, or by using Boris, you agree to it on behalf of your firm, and you confirm you're allowed to do that.

1. What Boris is

Boris is an AI employee for personal injury firms. You hire him for one or more jobs (for example Receptionist, Intake, Case Manager, Marketing, Ads Manager or Full Team). We set him up on your firm and run him for you as a service. The jobs you bought, and their prices, are shown at checkout and on your invoices.

2. Setup

  • We aim to have Boris live within 3 business days after you pay and finish the setup checklist. Some things depend on you or on others (your phone carrier, your case software, ad platforms granting access), and those can take longer.
  • Online orders pay a one-time launch fee for setup, unless it's waived (for example on annual plans).

3. Billing

  • Monthly plans are billed in advance each month and continue month to month.
  • Annual plans are billed in advance for 12 months.
  • Prices are a flat fee per job. We never charge a percentage of your fees, settlements or any case outcome.
  • Payments are processed by Stripe. If a payment fails, we'll let you know. If it isn't fixed within 14 days, we may pause Boris until it is.
  • We may change prices with at least 30 days' notice. Annual plans keep their price until renewal.
  • Prices don't include taxes. If tax applies, it's added to your invoice.

4. Cancelling and refunds

  • Monthly plans: cancel any time with 30 days' written notice (email is fine). You pay through the end of the notice period.
  • Annual plans: you can cancel within 14 days of payment for a refund prorated to the days used. After 14 days, annual plans are non-refundable, and Boris keeps working through the end of the year you paid for.
  • Launch fee: non-refundable once setup has started.

5. How Boris works for you

  • Boris acts on your firm's instructions and the rules you set for him.
  • He asks first before anything involving money, before any new ad, campaign or budget increase goes live, and before anything is sent in your firm's name that you haven't approved, such as a retainer or a demand. You can make him ask first about more things, any time.
  • He tells callers he is an AI assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed. You're responsible for any call-recording notices your state requires, and we'll help you set them up.
  • AI can make mistakes. Your team should review his work the way you'd review a new employee's, especially anything that goes to a client, a court or the other side.

6. Your firm's responsibilities

  • Legal advice is yours. Boris is not a law firm and never gives legal advice. He doesn't decide whether to take a case, negotiate, accept offers, or sign or file anything. Those decisions belong to your attorneys.
  • Your rules and your bar. You're responsible for following your state bar's rules, including rules on lawyer advertising, client solicitation, client communications and supervising non-lawyer assistants. That includes approving every ad before it goes live.
  • You'll give us accurate information and the access Boris needs (phone forwarding, calendars, case software, ad accounts), and keep your own logins secure.

7. Ads (Ads Manager job)

  • Your ad spend is paid by you directly to the ad platforms (Meta, Google and others). It is not part of our fee and never goes through us.
  • The ad platforms' own terms and decisions (for example rejecting an ad or limiting an account) are outside our control.

8. Your data

  • Your firm owns its data: your clients, calls, recordings, notes and files. We use it only to provide Boris to your firm. We don't sell it, and we don't use it to train AI models for anyone else.
  • When you leave, you can ask for an export of your data within 30 days. After that we delete it, except where the law requires us to keep it.
  • See our Privacy Policy for more.

9. Medical records and HIPAA

Boris handles medical records only after we've both signed a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) for your firm. The Paralegal job requires a signed BAA and isn't available through online checkout. Until a BAA is signed, please don't send medical records to Boris.

10. Confidentiality

Each of us will keep the other's confidential information private and use it only for this agreement. We treat everything about your clients and cases as confidential. This doesn't cover information that's already public, or that we must disclose by law (if that happens we'll tell you first, where allowed).

11. Acceptable use

You won't use Boris to break the law or your bar's rules, to send spam or unwanted calls or texts (including calls or texts without the consent the law requires), to harass anyone, or to try to copy, resell or reverse-engineer Boris without our written agreement.

12. No guarantees of results

We work hard to make Boris good at his jobs, but we don't guarantee any case outcome, number of cases, settlement amount, ad result or revenue. Boris is provided "as is", except for what this agreement expressly promises.

13. Limit of liability

To the extent the law allows, our total liability under this agreement is limited to the fees you paid us in the 3 months before the claim arose. Neither of us is liable for indirect or consequential damages, such as lost profits or lost cases. These limits don't apply to a breach of confidentiality or to amounts you owe us.

14. Ending the agreement

  • You can end it as described in section 4.
  • We can end it with 30 days' notice, or right away if you seriously break this agreement (for example, misuse under section 11 or non-payment after notice). If we end it without cause, we'll refund any prepaid fees for the unused period.
  • Sections 8, 10, 12, 13 and 15 still apply after the agreement ends.

15. General

  • This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. Any dispute goes to the state or federal courts in Texas.
  • We may update this agreement. We'll email you at least 30 days before material changes apply to you.
  • This agreement, your checkout order and any signed BAA are the whole agreement between us about Boris.
